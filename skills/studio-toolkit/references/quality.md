# Production and review standard

One focal subject per explanatory beat. Use semantic object IDs, progressive construction and a persistent spatial model. At the phrase explaining permeability, identify the venular wall and fluid crossing it; at leukocyte migration, identify the cell and its path. Repeating a narration sentence on a title slide is not an explanatory graphic.

Use one palette and type system. At 1080 reference height, start around 64–100 px for the main message and 34–44 px for required labels, then inspect at 360 px width. Arabic has no tracking; use a local shaped font such as Noto Sans Arabic with isolated English/chemical strings. Allow generous line height. Keep critical vertical content inside a central safe region, leaving space for platform controls and captions. Compare actual transformed bounds, not source CSS alone.

Entrances: ease-out cubic or restrained springs; exits typically faster. Camera: smooth focus/zoom on one world, then a readable hold. Default motion duration 0.3–0.7 s, stagger 0.06–0.12 s, reading hold at least 1 s plus roughly 0.25 s for each word beyond three. These are starting points, not rules against user intent. Linear motion is valid for constant velocity or measured scientific quantities. No mandatory grain, breathing, glow, or motion on every still. Do not add animation where it reduces comprehension.

Dim or gently defocus context while keeping the relevant label crisp. Trace the structure, build layers, use thin annotations and camera tracking. Avoid broad halos, heavy glow, irrelevant wipes and color ambiguity. Persistent objects should explain change across scenes. Particle movement and cell sizes in teaching diagrams are schematic, not quantitative measurements.

Speech leads sound. Use sparse licensed or procedurally generated effects tied to frame events. Measure integrated loudness and true peak on the final encoded file. Speech default target -16 LUFS, ceiling -1.5 dBTP before AAC; music ducking around 9 dB under voice. Listen for intelligibility, harshness, clipping, abrupt cuts and pacing. Automated loudness does not prove quality.

Render representative stills before a full master. Inspect the opening, every focus switch, scene seams, highest text density and last frame. Inspect full-size and mobile-size contact sheets. Check scientific referents, RTL, truncation, visual continuity and narration correspondence. Render again after material fixes. Automated schema/media tests are distinct from agent frame inspection and human creative/scientific approval. Report all three separately.
